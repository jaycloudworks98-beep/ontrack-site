Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

This policy explains what OnTrack does with the information you put into it. It is written to be read, not to be survived — if anything here is unclear, write to us and we will explain it.

The short version. Your business data is yours. We store it so the app can work, we do not sell it, we do not use it to advertise to you, and you can take it out or have it deleted whenever you want.

What we hold

  • Your business records — bills, parties, items, stock, godown entries, and the photos, videos and voice notes your staff attach to them.
  • Your account — the username, a one-way hash of your password (we never store the password itself), your name, and an email address if you gave one.
  • Staff PINs — stored as one-way hashes. Nobody at OnTrack can read a PIN.
  • Crash reports — when the app fails, it sends what went wrong so it can be fixed. These carry no bills, no parties and no photographs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising in OnTrack.
  • We do not read your bills, ledgers or photographs to build a profile of you.
  • We do not share one business's data with another. Every business is separated at the database level and the separation is enforced by the code on every single request.

Where it is kept

On managed servers with encryption in transit and at rest. Photos and videos are held in object storage; the database keeps only a link to them.

Artificial intelligence

Some features send text to an AI provider — reading a supplier's bill from a photograph, or answering a question you type. This happens only when you use those features, and only with the specific text or image needed. If you supply your own AI key, the request goes to your provider, under your account, and we are not in the middle of it.

Your rights

  • Export — take everything out, at any time, in a readable format.
  • Correct — edit anything in your records yourself.
  • Delete — ask us and your business and its data are removed within 30 days.

How long we keep it

Your records stay while your account exists — including if you stop paying, because losing a firm's ledger over a lapsed subscription would be indefensible. Photos and videos are cleared after 45 days by default to keep storage sane; the entries themselves remain. You can change or turn off that clearing in Settings.

Children

OnTrack is business software and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you inside the app before it takes effect — not by quietly editing this page.

Contact

Write to support@ontrackerp.app.
