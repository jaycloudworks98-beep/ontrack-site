Refund Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

Short and plain, because a refund policy that needs interpreting is not a policy.

Not satisfied in your first 7 days of paying? Write to us and we refund it in full. No form, no reason required.

Before you pay anything

You get 10 documents without registering, and 14 days free after registering. By the time any money changes hands you will have used OnTrack with your own items, your own dealers and your own godown. That is deliberate — we would rather you found out early than asked for a refund later.

After the first 7 days

Monthly subscriptions are not refunded for a part-used month. Cancel and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for; nothing renews after that.

If we are at fault

If the service is unusable for a stretch because of something on our side, tell us and we will credit that time. You should not pay for days you could not work.

How to ask

Write to support@ontrackerp.app from the email on your account, or message the support number in the app. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Your data after a refund

It stays. You can export it whenever you want, and it is only deleted if you ask.
