Support

Last updated 1 August 2026

OnTrack is built by people who run a wholesale business, which is why support is a person and not a ticket queue.

WhatsApp — fastest. Send a screenshot and what you were doing.

Email — support@ontrackerp.app

Hours — Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm IST. Something broken and stopping you billing gets answered the same day.

Before you write

Two things make a problem solvable in one message instead of five:

  • A screenshot of what you are seeing.
  • What you were doing just before it happened.

If the app itself has gone wrong, use Settings → Report a Bug — that sends us the technical details automatically, which is usually enough to find it without asking you anything.

Getting started

Setting up an ERP for the first time is the hardest part, so we do it with you. Tell us and we will help import your items, your dealers and your opening stock, and set up your staff and godown. There is no charge for this.

Training your staff

The godown app is deliberately built so there is nothing to learn — three buttons, big targets, Hindi where it matters. In practice godown staff are using it properly within a day. If yours are not, that is our problem to fix, so tell us.
