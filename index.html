Built for the godown, not the office

Most ERPs assume a desk and a keyboard. A godown is dusty, loud, and the person holding the phone has cloth in the other hand. OnTrack has three buttons on the home screen. That is the whole job.

1

Smart Search

Type "bedshet" and still find "Bedsheet — Double". Search by name, city or phone. The app understands typos, abbreviations and even Hindi transliteration. Your staff never get stuck at the search bar.

2

Voice Notes

When typing is too slow, your staff just speak. "50 bedsheets, double, cotton. Third bundle torn." The voice note attaches to the entry automatically. Your accountant listens and finishes the rest.

3

Photo Proof

Every entry carries a photograph taken on the spot. Rear camera, auto-compressed for weak signal. No entry is complete without visual proof. The dealer cannot say "I never received that."

4

Predictive Alerts

The 90-day bar glows red when a dealer is slipping. The AI flags overdue payments before they become write-offs. One user saved Rs 3 lakh last quarter just by making the call the app suggested.

5

Auto Approval Queue

Nothing moves stock until you say so. Entries wait in queue. The app tracks how long each has been waiting and turns red after one day. One tap to approve, one tap to reject.

6

Smart Offline Sync

No signal in the godown? Bills and entries save on the phone locally. The app syncs automatically when the line returns, with smart compression for low-bandwidth areas. Nothing is ever lost.

7

Business Insights

See who sells the most, which items are trending, and where money is stuck. Staff performance ranking, item sales by month, and receivables ageing — all calculated automatically, no spreadsheet needed.
